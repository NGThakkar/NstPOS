1. Overview

What is this task?

This task explains how a Cashier can sign in to CrazyPOS at the start of a shift. Logging in gives access to sales features, cart actions, and payment processing. It also helps track who performs each transaction.

When to use this task

Use this task at the beginning of each shift, after a system restart, or whenever you are logged out. You should also follow this process when switching from one cashier account to another.

Key benefits

  • Access cashier tools required for daily sales.
  • Keep transactions tied to the correct user account.
  • Start selling quickly with a consistent login process.

2. Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

  • [ ] An active Cashier account in CrazyPOS.
  • [ ] Your valid username and password.
  • [ ] Access to the CrazyPOS login page on your POS terminal.
  • [ ] A stable internet/network connection to the server.
  • [ ] A supported browser if using web access (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).

Required information to gather

  • Cashier username
  • Cashier password
  • Store or terminal assigned to your shift (if your branch uses multiple terminals)

3. Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Open the CrazyPOS login screen

Launch the app or website and confirm the login page is visible.

  1. Open CrazyPOS from the desktop shortcut or browser bookmark.
  2. Wait for the login page to load completely.
  3. Confirm that Username and Password fields are shown.

Tip: Save the login page in your browser favorites for faster access.

SCREENSHOT: CrazyPOS login page showing Username and Password fields

Step 2: Enter your cashier credentials

Type your account details carefully.

  1. Click the Username field and enter your cashier username.
  2. Click the Password field and enter your password.
  3. Recheck spelling and character case before continuing.

Important: Passwords are case-sensitive. Make sure Caps Lock is off.

SCREENSHOT: Username and Password fields filled in

Step 3: Sign in to your account

Submit the login form and wait for system confirmation.

  1. Click the Log In button.
  2. Wait a few seconds while the system validates your account.
  3. If prompted, choose your store/terminal and confirm.
SCREENSHOT: Post-login landing screen or terminal selection prompt

Step 4: Verify successful login

Check that you are on the cashier workspace and ready to start sales.

  1. Confirm your name or user badge appears in the top area.
  2. Verify the main POS screen is visible with cart and payment actions.
  3. Check that no login error message is displayed.
SCREENSHOT: Cashier dashboard/POS screen with logged-in user indicator

4. Expected Outcomes

After completing this task, you should see:

  • [ ] You are redirected from the login page to the main POS interface.
  • [ ] Your cashier name/account is visible in the session header.
  • [ ] Cart and payment actions are enabled for use.

Success indicators

  • A clear transition from login screen to cashier workspace.
  • No authentication error or warning banner after sign-in.

5. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: I see "Invalid username or password." What should I do?

A: Re-enter your username and password carefully and try again.

SCREENSHOT: Login error message for invalid credentials

Q: I cannot click the Log In button.

A: One or more required fields may be empty, or the page may not be fully loaded.

Steps to resolve:

  1. Confirm both Username and Password fields are filled.
  2. Refresh the page once.
  3. Re-enter credentials and click Log In again.

Q: The page keeps loading after I click Log In.

A: This usually indicates a network or server delay.

Note: If loading exceeds 30 seconds, check your network and notify support.

Common errors

Error Cause Solution
Invalid username or password Wrong credentials entered Re-enter carefully; reset password if needed
Account locked Too many failed login attempts Wait for unlock policy or contact supervisor/admin
Unable to connect to server Network or backend service unavailable Check network, then retry; escalate to IT if unresolved

7. Additional Notes & Tips

Best practices

  • Log in using only your assigned cashier account.
  • Verify you are on the correct terminal before starting transactions.
  • Log out at shift end to protect your session.

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Sharing credentials with another cashier.
  • Ignoring repeated login failures instead of reporting the issue.
