1. Overview

What is this task?

This task helps Manager users track stock levels and perform controlled quantity adjustments in CrazyPOS. It is structured to keep operations consistent, policy-aligned, and easy to audit.

When to use this task

Use this task during cycle checks, discrepancy resolution, or replenishment planning.

Key benefits

  • Maintain accurate on-hand quantities
  • Reduce stockouts and overstock
  • Support reliable purchase decisions

2. Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

  • [ ] Inventory module access is active
  • [ ] Current stock snapshot is available
  • [ ] Adjustment reason policy is known
  • [ ] Branch/store context selected

Required information to gather

  • Item/SKU list
  • Current and corrected quantity
  • Adjustment reason and note

3. Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Access the correct module

Open the inventory dashboard and load the adjustment workspace.

  1. Sign in with your Manager account.
  2. Open Inventory Dashboard and choose Stock Adjustment view.
  3. Confirm warehouse/store and date context.

Tip: Verify filters and context before editing any records.

SCREENSHOT: Landing screen for Monitor and Adjust Inventory

Step 2: Enter required details

Filter by category/location and inspect discrepancies

  1. Select the item/SKU requiring stock adjustment.
  2. Filter items by category/location and open discrepant SKUs.
  3. Enter corrected counts with adjustment reason.

Important: Incorrect values can cause policy or reconciliation issues.

SCREENSHOT: Data entry and selection fields for Monitor and Adjust Inventory

Step 3: Validate the result

Apply quantity adjustments with reason codes

  1. Review variance, projected stock, and validation warnings.
  2. Verify before/after stock values and variance impact.
  3. Resolve blocked adjustments or negative-stock warnings.
SCREENSHOT: Validation panel with totals and status checks

Step 4: Submit and confirm

Save and verify inventory stats update

  1. Confirm adjustment submission for selected items.
  2. Save adjustments and confirm updated on-hand quantities.
  3. Review stock KPIs for immediate anomalies.
