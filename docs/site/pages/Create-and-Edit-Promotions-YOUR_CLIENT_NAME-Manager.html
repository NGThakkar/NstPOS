1. Overview

What is this task?

This task explains how managers define, configure, and update promotion rules in CrazyPOS. Promotions can include date windows, eligibility conditions, and discount logic. Proper setup ensures checkout discounts are consistent and controlled.

When to use this task

Use this task when launching new campaigns, correcting promotion logic, or extending/ending promotional periods.

Key benefits

  • Improve campaign consistency across terminals.
  • Reduce incorrect discount application at checkout.
  • Enable controlled rollout of marketing offers.

Q: The system prevents submission.

A: Required fields, permissions, or policy checks may be incomplete.

Steps to resolve:

  1. Review highlighted required fields.
