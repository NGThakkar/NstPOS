1. Overview

What is this task?

This task explains how a Cashier starts a new sale in CrazyPOS, adds items to the cart, confirms totals, and completes payment. It is the core checkout workflow used for day-to-day customer transactions. Following these steps ensures sales are processed accurately and consistently.

When to use this task

Use this task whenever a customer is ready to purchase one or more items. It should be used for normal checkout flow after the cashier has logged in successfully.

Key benefits

  • Process customer purchases from cart to payment in one guided workflow.
  • Reduce billing errors by validating items, quantities, and totals before charging.
  • Capture transactions properly for reporting, receipts, and audit trails.

2. Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

  • [ ] Logged in using a valid Cashier account.
  • [ ] Access to the POS sales screen with cart and payment controls.
  • [ ] Products available in the system with valid prices.
  • [ ] Customer payment method ready (cash, card, or configured payment mode).
  • [ ] Printer configured if receipt printing is required.

Required information to gather

  • Item barcode or product name for each item the customer is buying
  • Quantity for each line item
  • Payment amount/tender details (cash amount received or card confirmation)

3. Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Open a new sales session

Start from the main POS screen and create a fresh transaction.

  1. Navigate to the Sales or POS screen after login.
  2. Click New Sale (or use the default blank cart if it opens automatically).
  3. Confirm the cart is empty before adding items.

Tip: If a previous cart is still visible, clear it first to avoid mixing transactions.

SCREENSHOT: POS main screen with New Sale action highlighted

Step 2: Add products to cart

Add all customer items and verify each line.

  1. Scan each item barcode, or search by product name and select it.
  2. Adjust quantity for any item where the customer buys more than one.
  3. Confirm item name, unit price, and line total are correct.
  4. Remove incorrect items from the cart if added by mistake.

Important: Check for duplicate scans before moving to payment.

SCREENSHOT: Cart with multiple items, quantity controls, and line totals

Step 3: Review totals and discounts

Validate the sale summary before charging the customer.

  1. Review subtotal, tax, discounts, and grand total.
  2. Apply eligible promotions according to store policy (if available in workflow).
  3. Confirm final payable amount with the customer.
SCREENSHOT: Cart summary panel showing subtotal, tax, discount, and total

Step 4: Complete payment

Collect payment and finalize the transaction.

  1. Click Proceed to Payment or Pay.
  2. Select the payment method (for example, Cash or Card).
  3. Enter received amount (for cash) or complete card confirmation.
  4. Click Complete Payment / Confirm.
SCREENSHOT: Payment modal with method selection and amount fields

Step 5: Confirm transaction completion

Ensure the sale is recorded and ready for receipt handling.

  1. Verify success message appears (for example, "Payment completed").
  2. Confirm a transaction ID/order number is generated.
  3. Print or preview receipt if required.
SCREENSHOT: Payment success message with transaction ID and receipt options

4. Expected Outcomes

After completing this task, you should see:

  • [ ] The transaction is saved successfully in the system.
  • [ ] A transaction ID/order reference is displayed.
  • [ ] Cart is cleared or reset for the next customer.
  • [ ] Receipt option is available (print or reprint, based on setup).

Success indicators

  • Payment status is marked as completed/paid.
  • The completed sale appears in transaction records.

5. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: An item does not appear when scanned.

A: The barcode may not be mapped or the item may be inactive.

SCREENSHOT: Product search/scan area with no match result

Q: The total amount looks incorrect.

A: Quantity, item selection, or pricing/promotion rules may be causing a mismatch.

Steps to resolve:

  1. Recheck each line item's quantity and price.
  2. Remove and re-add suspicious line items.
  3. Reapply discounts/promotions and review totals again.

Q: Payment fails or does not complete.

A: This can happen due to invalid amount input, device issues, or temporary server/network problems.

Note: Retry once after validating payment details, then escalate if still failing.

Common errors

Error Cause Solution
Product not found Barcode not registered or wrong code scanned Search by name; verify barcode mapping with admin
Amount tendered is less than total Received amount entered incorrectly Enter correct tender amount and retry
Could not save transaction Temporary server/network issue Check connection, retry payment once, contact support if repeated

7. Additional Notes & Tips

Best practices

  • Confirm totals aloud with the customer before tapping Complete Payment.
  • Keep scanner and payment devices connected and tested before peak hours.
  • Process one customer at a time per terminal to avoid cart confusion.

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Completing payment without reviewing quantity changes.
  • Applying manual adjustments without supervisor approval.
