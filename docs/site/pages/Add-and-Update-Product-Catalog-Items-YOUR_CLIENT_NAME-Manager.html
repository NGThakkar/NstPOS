1. Overview

What is this task?

This task helps Manager users create new products and maintain existing product details in CrazyPOS. It is structured to keep operations consistent, policy-aligned, and easy to audit.

When to use this task

Use this task when onboarding new SKUs or correcting product metadata.

Key benefits

  • Keep POS search and scanning accurate
  • Improve pricing consistency
  • Reduce checkout and inventory errors

2. Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

  • [ ] Manager catalog permissions are active
  • [ ] Category structure exists
  • [ ] SKU/barcode standards are known
  • [ ] Pricing/tax rules are available

Required information to gather

  • SKU/barcode
  • Product name and category
  • Selling price, tax, and status

3. Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Access the correct module

Open catalog management and choose the add or edit product screen.

  1. Sign in with your Manager account.
  2. Open Product Catalog and choose Add Item or Edit Item.
  3. Confirm the selected branch/catalog scope.

Tip: Verify filters and context before editing any records.

SCREENSHOT: Landing screen for Add and Update Product Catalog Items

Step 2: Enter required details

Enter product identifiers, names, category, and pricing

  1. Choose Add Product or open an existing product for edit.
  2. Enter SKU/barcode, name, category, and pricing fields.
  3. Set tax profile, unit, and active status.

Important: Incorrect values can cause policy or reconciliation issues.

SCREENSHOT: Data entry and selection fields for Add and Update Product Catalog Items

Step 3: Validate the result

Validate barcode uniqueness and tax setup

  1. Review uniqueness and validation messages for product data.
  2. Check barcode uniqueness and duplicate product warnings.
  3. Validate category assignment and tax settings.
SCREENSHOT: Validation panel with totals and status checks

Step 4: Submit and confirm

Save changes and verify product appears in POS search

  1. Click Save Product to commit catalog changes.
  2. Confirm save success and item visibility in search.
  3. Test scan/search behavior on POS terminal.
SCREENSHOT: Success message with generated reference
