1. Overview

What is this task?

This task explains how a Cashier applies backend-validated promotions while processing a sale in CrazyPOS. Promotions are calculated on the server to ensure policy compliance and consistent discount rules. This helps prevent manual pricing mistakes and unauthorized discounts.

When to use this task

Use this task during checkout after items are added to the cart and before final payment is completed. It is commonly used when customers qualify for campaign discounts, bundle offers, or rule-based price reductions.

Key benefits

  • Apply discount rules consistently using server-side logic.
  • Reduce pricing errors and unauthorized discount usage.
  • Keep transaction totals accurate for reporting and audits.

2. Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

  • [ ] Logged in as a Cashier with checkout access.
  • [ ] An active cart with one or more items.
  • [ ] Backend promotion engine available and connected.
  • [ ] Valid product prices and quantities in cart.
  • [ ] Store policy visibility for restricted discounts.

Required information to gather

  • Customer-eligible promotion details (if provided by policy or campaign)
  • Cart items and quantities that may trigger promotions
  • Manager details for approval flow when restricted discounts are involved

3. Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the cart for promotion check

Make sure cart content is final enough for promotion calculation.

  1. Add all intended items to the cart.
  2. Update quantities for each line item as needed.
  3. Verify there are no duplicate or incorrect items.

Tip: Promotion eligibility often depends on exact quantities and item combinations.

SCREENSHOT: Cart screen with item list and quantity controls

Step 2: Request server-side promotion evaluation

Trigger the backend pricing/discount preview from checkout.

  1. Click Preview Pricing, Recalculate, or Apply Promotions (label may vary by screen).
  2. Wait for the system to return server-calculated totals.
  3. Review promotion lines shown in the summary panel.

Important: Do not finalize payment until server totals are returned successfully.

SCREENSHOT: Promotion preview action and updated totals area

Step 3: Validate applied discounts

Confirm that discounts are expected and policy-compliant.

  1. Check each applied promotion name and discount amount.
  2. Compare the adjusted total with expected campaign/store rules.
  3. Remove or retry promotion check if the result appears incorrect.
SCREENSHOT: Cart summary with subtotal, discount entries, and final total

Step 4: Handle restricted discounts (if prompted)

Follow approval flow when discount requires elevated authorization.

  1. If the system prompts for approval, pause checkout.
  2. Initiate manager approval request in the discount prompt.
  3. Wait for approval confirmation before proceeding.
SCREENSHOT: Restricted discount approval prompt/modal

Step 5: Continue to payment

Proceed with payment only after promotion totals are accepted.

  1. Confirm final payable amount with the customer.
  2. Click Proceed to Payment.
  3. Complete payment using the standard checkout flow.
SCREENSHOT: Final total accepted and payment action enabled

4. Expected Outcomes

After completing this task, you should see:

  • [ ] Eligible promotions are reflected in the cart summary.
  • [ ] Final total updates based on server-side validation.
  • [ ] Restricted discounts are blocked until approval is granted.
  • [ ] Checkout can proceed using approved final totals.

Success indicators

  • Promotion source is clearly shown as server-calculated.
  • Transaction completes using the discounted total without override errors.

5. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: No promotions were applied, but the customer says they are eligible.

A: Eligibility may depend on item mix, quantity thresholds, date/time, or customer rules.

SCREENSHOT: Promotion panel showing no applicable discounts

Q: I see an approval-required message for discount.

A: The discount is restricted by policy and requires manager authorization.

Steps to resolve:

  1. Click Request Approval in the prompt.
  2. Ask the manager to review and approve in their flow.
  3. Re-run promotion check after approval is granted.

Q: Promotion values changed after cart edit.

A: Server-side promotions are recalculated whenever relevant cart details change.

Note: Reconfirm totals with the customer after any cart update.

Common errors

Error Cause Solution
Promotion service unavailable Backend service/network interruption Retry after connection check; continue without promo only if policy allows
Approval required for discount Discount exceeds cashier permissions Request manager approval and re-apply
Pricing mismatch after update Cart changed after last calculation Recalculate promotions before payment

7. Additional Notes & Tips

Best practices

  • Always trigger server recalculation after cart edits.
  • Confirm discount lines with the customer before charging.
  • Escalate repeated promotion errors to support instead of manual guesswork.

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Completing payment before promotion response is finalized.
  • Manually assuming a discount amount without backend confirmation.
